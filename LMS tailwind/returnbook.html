Return Desk Guidelines

When receiving returned books at the physical desk, please verify:

  • Inspect the physical textbook condition for any missing pages, water damage, or binding wear.
  • Check the original due date to calculate any late penalties (₹10 per day overdue).
  • Submitting this form automatically increments the available inventory count back up in the main catalog.
